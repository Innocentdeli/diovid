To produce your content video in Diovid, follow this step-by-step workflow. The platform uses AI to transform your ideas into professional videos through an intuitive 4-step process (Cast → Script → Audio → Video) with professional-grade post-production tools.

🚀 Quick Start Guide
Step 1: Access the Director Workspace
Start the app: Run npm run dev in your terminal
Navigate to Director: Go to http://localhost:3000/director in your browser
You'll see four main tabs: Cast, Script, Audio, and Preview
Step 2: Create Your Cast (Characters)
Add Characters: Click the "+" button to add actors to your story
Upload Photos: For each character, upload a photo (JPG/PNG)
The AI will automatically extract their persona, vibe, and traits
This creates realistic character profiles for the AI director
Name Your Characters: Give each character a name
Review AI Analysis: The system analyzes the photo and suggests personality traits
Step 3: Write Your Script
Switch to Script Tab: Click the "Script" tab
Use the AI Writer: The rich text editor has AI co-writing features:
Rewrite: Improve selected text
Continue: Extend your story
Improve: Enhance writing quality
Scriptify: Convert story to screenplay format
Global Scriptify: Transform entire story into production script
Write Your Story: Describe your plot, dialogue, and scenes
AI Assistance: Select text and use AI tools to enhance your writing
Step 4: Produce Audio (Audio-First Workflow)
Switch to Audio Tab: Click the "Audio" tab
Open the Audio Studio: Click "Open Audio Studio" to access the MultiModal interface
Record Voices: Record character dialogue and voiceovers
Voice Synthesis: Generate AI character voices based on personality traits
Music Generation: Create custom background music for your scenes
Audio Mixing: Professional audio mixing and track management
Audio Timeline: Organize and edit audio tracks with precise timing
Complete or Skip: You can skip audio (optional) and proceed directly to video
Step 5: Direct Your Video
Switch to Preview Tab: Click the "Preview" tab
Review Your Setup: Ensure you have characters with photos and a script
Click "Produce Stream": This triggers the AI orchestration process
Step 6: AI Production Process
The system will automatically:

Analyze your script and break it into scenes
Generate backgrounds for each scene using AI image generation
Create character videos with appropriate emotions and gestures
Add camera movements and cinematic effects
Generate voice synthesis for dialogue (if enabled in audio step)
Mix audio tracks from the MultiModal studio
Sync video timing with audio tracks (audio-first approach)
Step 7: Professional Post-Production (NEW)
Access Production Studio: In the preview panel, find the "Professional Production Studio" section
Choose from 9 professional tools organized by category:

**Video Editing:**
- Color Grading: Professional color correction with presets (cinematic, warm, cool, vintage)
- Visual Effects: Motion blur, depth of field, particles, vignette, chromatic aberration
- Green Screen: Chroma key with background replacement (solid, blur, or custom image)

**Audio Processing:**
- Audio Effects: EQ, compression, reverb, noise gate, de-esser, audio normalization
- Quick presets: Voice enhance and music boost

**Animation & Motion:**
- Full Body Animation: Chest-up and full-body modes, walking, interactions
- Multi-character interaction: Position characters, set spacing, manage scene dynamics

**Quality Control:**
- Format Converter: Output MP4, WebM, MOV, GIF, PNG with bitrate optimization
- Platform presets: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Archive quality settings
- Resolution options: 720p, 1080p, 2K, 4K with custom framerate and codec selection

**Collaboration:**
- Version Control: Git-style branching, commit messages, version history
- Asset Library: Organize and manage backgrounds, characters, audio, effects
- Team Review: Comments, feedback threads, resolved vs. unresolved issues, team presence

Step 8: Preview & Export
Watch the Preview: See your video in the cinematic viewport
Use Controls:
Play/pause the performance
View scene-by-scene breakdown
Access the floating MultiModal icon for audio adjustments
Export Options:
Choose format (MP4, GIF, PNG) with professional encoding
Select quality presets or custom bitrate
Download your finished video
